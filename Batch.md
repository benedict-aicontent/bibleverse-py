# DAY 1 — PROVERBS 4:23

### 1. Emotional Hook

Everything you do flows from one place. Are you guarding it?

### 2. Bible Verse & Bible Text

**Proverbs 4:23 (WEB)**

Keep your heart with all diligence, for out of it is the wellspring of life.

### 3. Reflection

What you let settle in your heart eventually shapes your words, choices, and direction. Guarding it isn't about building walls; it's about noticing what you feed it each day. Be careful what you watch, hear, and dwell on, because a well-kept heart keeps life flowing clean.

### 4. YouTube Title

### 5. YouTube Description

Proverbs 4:23 reminds us that the heart is the source, not just a feeling. The thoughts we welcome and the things we dwell on slowly shape the people we become, which is why Scripture asks us to keep it with diligence.

If this verse speaks to your heart today, carry its message with you. Share it with someone who might need this reminder, and stay connected for more daily encouragement through God's Word.
